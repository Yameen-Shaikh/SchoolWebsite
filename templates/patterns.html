Patterns

Teacher-friendly pattern practice for young learners. Use this page as a starting point for your school’s handwriting program—print or follow the steps during lessons.

Pattern 1: Straight Lines

Goal: steady direction and spacing. Trace slowly, then copy 5–10 lines.

Age: 3–5+ Session: 5 min Teacher cue: “Go slow.”

Pattern 2: Curves

Goal: smooth turning. Start at the dot, move smoothly, and stop where you begin.

Age: 4–6+ Session: 5–7 min Teacher cue: “Smooth like a ribbon.”

Pattern 3: Mix & Match

Goal: combining shapes for letter practice. Connect straight + curve strokes carefully.

Age: 5–7+ Session: 7–10 min Teacher cue: “One shape at a time.”

Teacher Tip

Classroom routine: (1) demonstrate slowly, (2) trace together, (3) copy independently. Use positive feedback—small steps build big confidence.
