Our classroom routine

Three simple steps that keep lessons consistent and help children learn with confidence.

1

Warm-up tracing

Move slowly over the line so children build direction and control.

2

Guided copying

Copy a few lines at a time—celebrate effort, not speed.

3

Independent repetition

Repeat for smoother hand movement and lasting confidence.

Teacher Tip

Demonstrate slowly → trace together → copy independently. Use kind encouragement so every child feels proud of progress.
